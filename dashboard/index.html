The model

implied = round(TVL × category multiple × growth × maturity × chains)

  • Category multiple. For each category, the median mcap/TVL of its protocols whose token already trades, shrunk toward the global median (0.25× across live tokens) with a prior weight of 5, and clamped to [0.02, 1.0]. A category with no live tokens takes the global median.
  • CEX excluded. Exchange tokens trade against user balances, not protocol TVL, so they carry no multiple for a protocol launch.
  • Growth. The protocol's 7-day TVL change, clamped to [−60, 120] and counted at half weight: a hot week is a hint, not a value.
  • Maturity. 0.6 for a protocol listed yesterday, rising to 1.0 at three years old; an unknown listing date counts as one year.
  • Chains. Each chain past the first adds 2%, up to five extra.
  • No name is special-cased. The same formula scores all 758 protocols in the snapshot.
